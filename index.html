Storm damage

Hail or wind hit your roof?

Storm damage isn't always visible from the ground, and insurance policies often set time limits for filing a claim. Get it checked before a small problem turns into a leak.

How We Handle Storm Damage
  1. InspectWe check shingles, vents, flashing, and gutters for hail and wind damage.
  2. DocumentYou get a dated photo report to share with your insurance company.
  3. Meet the adjusterIf you'd like, we'll be on-site when your insurer's adjuster inspects.
  4. RestoreOnce approved, we rebuild the roof to the same standard as any other job.
