Our story

Why we started Steel Creed.

Steel Creed Roofing & Construction is a newly established, locally owned company based in La Grange Park. We started it because homeowners and property owners deserve a roofer who shows up when promised, explains the job honestly, and does the work right.

The company is new, but the experience isn't. Our team brings more than 10 years of hands-on roofing work on Chicago-area homes and commercial buildings, and that experience shapes how we bid, build, and clean up every job.

When you work with Steel Creed, you deal directly with the people responsible for your roof. No call centers, no handoffs.

The Steel Creed

Five promises on every job.

Our name is our code. These are the standards we work by on every roof, no matter the size.

  1. 01

    Tell the truth about your roof.

    If it needs a repair and not a replacement, we say so. You get photos, not pressure.

  2. 02

    The price we quote is the price you pay.

    Itemized, written quotes. Possible extras, like rotted decking, are priced up front.

  3. 03

    Do it to code, every layer.

    Ice and water shield, drip edge, proper nailing, and real ventilation, including the parts nobody sees.

  4. 04

    Respect your property and your neighbors.

    Protected landscaping, a daily cleanup, and a magnetic sweep for nails before we leave.

  5. 05

    Stand behind the work.

    We walk the finished job with you and stay reachable long after the dumpster leaves.

The name

Steel is what we build with: strength, durability, and work that holds up to Illinois weather. Creed is how we work: a set of promises we keep on every job. Together they're the reason behind our motto, Protecting What Matters. Building What Lasts.
