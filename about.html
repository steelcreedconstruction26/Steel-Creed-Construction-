Our story

Not just another name in roofing.

Steel Creed Roofing & Construction wasn't created to be just another name in the roofing industry. It was founded on a straightforward belief: homeowners deserve honest answers, quality workmanship, and contractors who stand behind their word.

We're a locally owned company based in La Grange Park, Illinois, serving homeowners and businesses throughout the Chicago area.

While Steel Creed is a new company, the experience behind it is not. Our leadership brings years of hands-on roofing experience, practical construction knowledge, and an understanding of what it takes to get a job done properly.

We believe a company's reputation isn't built by what it promises. It's built by what it delivers.

The name

Our name is our commitment.

STEEL represents strength, durability, and the expectation that our work should withstand the test of time.

CREED represents the principles we choose to operate by: integrity, accountability, craftsmanship, and keeping our word.

Those aren't just words we want printed on a website. They're the standards we're building this company around.

We believe in doing what we say, communicating clearly, respecting our customers' properties, and taking responsibility for the work we perform.
