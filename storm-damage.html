Warning signs

Signs your roof took a hit.

If you notice any of these after a storm, schedule an inspection. Small damage can turn into leaks months later.

  • Dents in gutters, downspouts, vents, or window screens
  • Shingle granules piling up in gutters or at downspouts
  • Missing, creased, or lifted shingles
  • Dark spots or bruising on shingles
  • New water stains on ceilings or in the attic
  • Neighbors getting roof inspections or replacements

Storm FAQ

Insurance questions, answered.

Do you help with insurance claims?

We document the damage with a dated photo report and can meet your adjuster on-site. Your claim and its decisions stay between you and your insurance company; we provide the documentation and the roof.

Is there a deadline to file a claim?

Many policies set a time limit for reporting storm damage. Check your policy or call your insurance agent, and get an inspection as soon as you can.

Will an inspection cost me anything?

No. Storm inspections are free and there is no obligation.

What if the inspection finds no damage?

Then we'll tell you that, show you the photos, and you're done. No pressure.
