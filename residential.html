Homeowner FAQ

Straight answers.

How do I know if I need a new roof or just a repair?

Look for curling or missing shingles, granules collecting in your gutters, water stains on ceilings, or a roof more than 20 years old. A free inspection is the easiest way to know for sure.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most homes are done in one to two days, depending on size, steepness, weather, and how much decking needs replacing.

Do I need to be home during the work?

No. We'll walk the job with you before we start and after we finish. Move cars out of the driveway and keep pets inside while we work.

What does an estimate cost?

Nothing. Inspections and written estimates are free, with no obligation.
